Seldom & Shade

Seldom and Shade is an arcade twin stick action game, where you play as Seldom, the ghost, and his dog, Shade at the same time with one joystick each. You navigate in a nightmarish field, trying to evade and destroy enemies by luring them into your characters' ethereally strong bond (the 'leash' between Seldom and Shade). But beware! Some of your foes have means to cut this bond!

Solo developer (student project)

I made this game for Block A of Year 2 during my studies at Breda University of Applied Sciences. As I was specializing in tech design, my task was to build on top of a twin-stick shooter template. I wanted to make something that's unique and has a clean and simple design, and so I settled on making a game where the player has to simultaneously control two characters with each joystick. Surprisingly the unconventional controls did not turn out to be as jarring as I anticipated and the game turned out be exactly what an arcade game should be: people were excited to play, jumping back to reach a new high-score and had fun in the meantime.

  • I also conducted some playtests
  • EXAMPLE! Players played defensively as opposed to my intention. This was because if they stretched the leash, their characters ended up being too close to the edge of the screen and an enemy could kill them unexpectedly. My solution was to implement arrows on the edge of the screen that indicated nearby off-screen enemies.
